WHAT WE DISCOVERED TODAY

Two questions, a few formulas, used everywhere

Rectangle

P = 2 × (l + b)
A = l × b

Square

P = 4 × a
A = a × a = a²

Triangle

A = ½ × b × h
half of the rectangle around it

Rhombus

A = ½ × d₁ × d₂
half of the rectangle around it

Perimeter = a length

m, cm, km
how far it is all the way around

Area = a surface

m², cm², km²
how many unit squares cover it
Why perimeter? To know how much fence, border, lace, toran, fairy-lights or skirting we need. Anything that goes around.
Why area? To know how much paint, tiles, carpet, grass, fertiliser or land we need. Anything that covers a surface.

Same perimeter ≠ same area. The rule (formula) comes from counting smartly, not from magic.
