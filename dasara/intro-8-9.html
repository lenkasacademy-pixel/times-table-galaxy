WHAT WE DISCOVERED TODAY

Faces you can cover, space you can fill

Cuboid · surface

SA = 2(lb + bh + hl)
3 pairs of twin faces

Cuboid · 4 walls

LSA = 2(l + b) × h
perimeter of the floor × height

Cuboid · volume

V = l × b × h
base area × height

Cube · surface

SA = 6a²
4 walls only: 4a²

Cube · volume

V = a³
double the side → 8 × the volume

Units

surface m² · space m³
1 m³ = 1000 litres
Why surface area? To know how much paint, tiles, wrapping paper, cardboard or metal sheet we need. Anything that covers the faces.
Why volume? To know how much water a tank holds, how much sand a truck carries, how big an AC a room needs. Anything that fills the space.

Same volume ≠ same surface. Compact shapes have the least surface for their space.
