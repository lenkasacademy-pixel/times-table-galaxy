Things in Rings

Crack all three rules

Pick one rule for each ring. All three right and you win on the spot. Any wrong and you draw 2 cards and can't solve again.

How to play

  1. The app secretly picks a rule for each ring, like “is food or drink” or “has the letter R”. Your job is to work them out.
  2. Two things start on the board as free hints.
  3. On your turn, tap a card in your hand, then tap the zone where you think it belongs: inside one ring, where rings overlap, or outside all of them.
  4. Right? It stays there with a green glow. Wrong? The app slides it to its true zone (a free clue for everyone) and you draw 1 card.
  5. Think you've cracked it? Instead of playing a card, tap Solve and pass the iPad. Pick the rule for each ring from 6 choices. All three right = you win! Any wrong = you draw 2 cards and can't solve again.
  6. Empty your hand to win. If the draw pile runs out, the player with the fewest cards wins.
