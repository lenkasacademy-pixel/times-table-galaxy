How to play

  1. Teams take turns. Each round, one player from the playing team is the Psychic.
  2. A card shows two ends of a scale, like 🧍 Could happen in real life ↔ 🦸 Only possible in Telugu cinema.
  3. Everyone else looks away. The Psychic peeks at the hidden target on the dial.
  4. The Psychic gives one clue that sits right at that spot. Target far right? Maybe “Hero stops a train with one finger”.
  5. The team argues it out and drags the needle. Then they lock it.
  6. Rival steal: the next team guesses if the target is left or right of the needle. Correct = +1. A bullseye blocks the steal.
  7. Reveal! Points depend on how close the needle landed:
🎯 Pink = 4Gold = 3Teal = 2

🌶️ Masala twists pop up randomly: dumb charades clues, mother-tongue only, sharp-shooter and more. 💥 Each team also has one Dhamaka token to double a round. First to the target score takes the crown!
