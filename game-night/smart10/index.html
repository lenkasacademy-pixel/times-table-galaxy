How to play

  1. Each round, the dial shows one question with 10 answers hidden around it.
  2. Players take turns in order. On your turn, either pick a number on the dial and answer it, or pass.
  3. Right answer: you win a glowing token for this round and stay in. The next player goes.
  4. Wrong answer: you lose every token from this round and sit out till the round ends.
  5. Pass: you bank your tokens as points and sit out the rest of the round. Safe, but you stop earning.
  6. The round ends when everyone is out or has passed, or all 10 answers are open. Players still in bank their tokens. Then every hidden answer is shown.
  7. The next round starts with the next player. When someone reaches the target score at the end of a round, the highest score wins. A tie at the top means one more round.

👍 Yes/no and 🔘 pick-one questions: tap your answer. 🔢 Number questions: type it on the keypad. 🗣️ Say-it questions: say your answer out loud first, then reveal, and the other players judge it.
