How to play

  1. Each round, the 16 cards are shuffled and 1 is set aside face-down. With 2 players, 3 more are set aside face-up. Everyone gets 1 secret card.
  2. On your turn the iPad comes to you. Tap “show me my cards”, you draw 1, then play 1 of your 2 cards face-up and do what it says.
  3. Keep your hand secret. Priest and Baron results show only on your screen, then tap Hide & pass.
  4. You're out of the round if a Guard names your card, you lose a Baron duel, or you discard the Princess. A Handmaid keeps you safe until your next turn.
  5. The Countess must be played if your other card is the King or Prince.
  6. The round ends when only one player is left, or the deck runs out. Then everyone still in reveals: highest card wins (tie: highest total of played cards).
  7. The round winner gets a wax-sealed love letter ❤️ and starts the next round. First to 7 / 5 / 4 letters (2 / 3 / 4 players) wins the Princess's heart!
