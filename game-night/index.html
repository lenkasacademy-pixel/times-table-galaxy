Game Night

Six party games for 3–4 players, all on one iPad.

📻

Dimaag Milao

2–4 teams · 30 min

One clue, one dial. The Psychic gives a clue and the team moves the needle to the hidden target.

Play 🍀

So Clover!

3–6 players · best 3–4 · team game

Each player writes four secret clues on a clover. The table rebuilds it together by matching word cards to the clues.

Play 🔟

Smart10

2–8 players · best 3–4

Ten answers around one question. Answer one to keep going, or pass and bank your points. One wrong answer and you lose that round's points.

Play ✖️

Cross Clues

2–6 players · best 4 · team game

A grid of words. Peek at a secret square, give one word that links its row and column, and see if the team finds it.

Play ⭕

Things in Rings

2–6 players · best 4

Three rings, three secret rules. Place things in the rings, learn from every mistake, and crack all three rules first. Includes a numbers mode for maths class.

Play 💌

Love Letter

2–4 players · best 4

Sixteen cards, one palace. Pass the iPad, bluff, deduce, and get your letter to the Princess.

Play
