How to play

  1. Everyone is on one team. You win or lose together.
  2. Clue time. Pass the iPad around. On your secret turn you get 4 word cards in the middle of a clover. Turn cards with ⟳, or swap up to 2 cards for fresh ones.
  3. Each leaf touches two words. Type one word on the leaf that links them: Mango + Monsoon → Summer. You can't use the words themselves.
  4. Guess time. One clover at a time. Its author stays silent 🤫. The team sees the 4 clues and gets 5 cards: the 4 real ones plus a decoy, all turned randomly.
  5. Tap a card, then tap a slot. Tap ⟳ to turn a card. Tap a placed card to send it back. Press Check when all 4 slots are full.
  6. Scoring: all 4 right (spot and direction) on the first try = 6. Otherwise right cards lock in and you get one more try: all 4 = 4, else 1 point per right card.
  7. When every clover is solved, see your team rating!
