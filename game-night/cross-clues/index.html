How to play

  1. The board has words along the top (A, B, C…) and down the side (1, 2, 3…). Every square is where one top word crosses one side word.
  2. Every square has one secret card. Each player holds 2 cards (1 card with 5–6 players). You all play as one team.
  3. On your turn, take the iPad privately and pick one of your cards, like B3.
  4. Think of ONE word that links both words of that square. Say it out loud (you can also type it so it shows big).
Chai×Train→“Vendor”
  1. Everyone else talks it out and taps the square they think it is. Right? The square turns gold. Wrong? That card's square is lost and stays dark.
  2. Draw a new card and pass the iPad on. The game ends when every square is gold or lost. Fill as much gold as you can!

🤐 Clue rules: one word only, no words from the board, no pointing or acting. The clue-giver stays silent while others guess.
